Privacy policy

Prelaunch information · October 23, 2026

Appweld is currently a prelaunch website and frontend demonstration. At this stage, the public preview does not send website URLs to an Appweld scanning or build service and does not create user accounts.

Information entered into the preview

The branding preview changes on your device. The URL you submit is passed in the address of the next page to display a local demonstration; please do not enter private or secret URLs. Do not include personal credentials or tokens in website links.

Hosting and technical data

Hosting infrastructure may process technical connection information such as IP address, browser headers, and request logs for security and delivery. External fonts may cause your browser to contact the font provider. No advertising tracker is deliberately installed in this version.

Before paid launch

App generation will require limited build data, transaction records and temporary output files. This policy will be updated with specific storage periods, payment providers, data-deletion processes and business contact details before any paid checkout goes live.
