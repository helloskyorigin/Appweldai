Acceptable use

Prelaunch information · October 23, 2026

Appweld is designed for people creating apps from websites they own or are explicitly authorized to use.

Do not submit

  • Websites that you do not own and have no permission to convert.
  • Phishing, malware, impersonation, fraudulent services, or other illegal content.
  • Private network addresses, credentials, access tokens, or secret administrative URLs.
  • Content that violates third-party intellectual property or platform rules.

App store requirements

Google Play applies additional rules to WebView apps, repetitive or low-functionality apps and certain payment or account flows. Builders are responsible for obtaining all necessary rights and meeting store policies.

Enforcement

When a production build service is available, technical safeguards and transaction review may be used to prevent misuse. This prelaunch preview does not run a build service.
